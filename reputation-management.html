FAQ

Review & Reputation Management questions

Can you remove negative reviews?

We can't remove legitimate reviews, but we respond professionally, help resolve the issue, and flag reviews that violate platform policies.

Is asking for reviews allowed?

Yes — Google encourages businesses to ask customers for reviews. We follow each platform's guidelines (for example, Yelp discourages soliciting reviews, so we use a compliant approach there).

How do review requests get sent?

After a job is marked complete, your customer automatically receives a friendly text or email with a direct link to leave a review.
