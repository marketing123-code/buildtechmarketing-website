FAQ

Facebook & Instagram Ads questions

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your market and goals. Many local service businesses start between $1,000 and $3,000 per month in ad spend. We'll recommend a budget during your strategy call.

Is ad spend included in the management fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta from your own account, so you keep full ownership and transparency.

How fast will I see leads?

Campaigns typically start generating leads within the first 1–2 weeks, then improve as we optimize.
