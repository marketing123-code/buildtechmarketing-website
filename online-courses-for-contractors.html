The courses

Three courses. Every number on the job covered.

From the first bid to the final invoice, learn the skills that decide whether a project makes money.

Construction Estimating

Work out what a project will cost before construction starts.

  • Calculating material and labor costs
  • Preparing estimates and bids
  • Analyzing the total project cost
  • Reducing errors in your calculations
Why it matters: Helps you plan budgets, submit competitive bids and protect your profit.

Cost Control

Monitor and manage spending while the project is being built.

  • Comparing actual costs against the estimate
  • Monitoring the budget
  • Spotting cost overruns early
  • Forecasting future costs
Why it matters: Helps you avoid financial losses and keep every project profitable.

Quantity Takeoffs

Measure materials and work quantities directly from the construction drawings.

  • Calculating concrete, rebar and excavation
  • Measuring areas and volumes
  • Identifying material quantities
  • Building quantity schedules
Why it matters: Ensures accurate material orders, reduces waste and forms the basis of an accurate estimate.
